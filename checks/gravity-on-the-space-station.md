# Verification: Gravity on the space station

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Equivalence principle now stated for a small room over a short time (tidal effects otherwise distinguish the cases): https://www.astronomy.ohio-state.edu/weinberg.21/A142/notes6.pdf
- Microgravity: about 1 µg is the quasi-steady level only; causes now include rotation; vibration and thrusters reach milli-g: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20090014810/downloads/20090014810.pdf
- Drop rides: weightlessness during the fall, not "at the top", and not "rise out of your seat" (true only on powered towers): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_time_(rides)
- Parabolic flights: arcs fly at roughly 7 to 10 km, so "less than 10 km up"; the aircraft follows a free-fall path with thrust cancelling drag: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced-gravity_aircraft
- Moon: "exactly" → "just" (agreement about 1%; barycentre and solar perturbations).
- "Weightless" glossed as zero apparent weight (NZ and IB define weight as mg).

## Verified
- (6370/6770)² = 0.885; 6% further from the centre; half gravity at about 2,640 km up: derivation.
- Orbital speed 7.67 km/s, period 92 min: derivation; https://coolcosmos.ipac.caltech.edu/ask/282-How-fast-does-the-Space-Station-travel-
- Auckland to Wellington 493 km great circle, more than the ISS altitude: derivation.
- Basketball model 0.75 cm; geostationary 2.3%; Moon distance 0.027%: derivation.
- Moon 384,000 km, Neptune 4.5 billion km: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/
- Einstein 1907, "happiest thought": https://www.astronomy.ohio-state.edu/weinberg.21/A142/notes6.pdf
- NASA prefers "microgravity": https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/for-educators/what-is-microgravity/
- Vomit Comet, about 25 s per parabola: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced-gravity_aircraft
- Leaky cup demo: https://physicslabs.colorado.edu/demos/mechanics/newtons-second-law/accelerated-reference-frames/leaking-cup-drop/
