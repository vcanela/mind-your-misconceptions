# Verification: Irradiated strawberries

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Tagline limited to gamma rays and X-rays (neutrons and very energetic particles can induce activity, as the entry explains).
- Tracer patients are not "contaminated": the IAEA defines contamination as unintended or undesirable. Reworded in two places: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radioactive_contamination
- Radura: often used, required in some countries; in New Zealand a written statement is required and the symbol is optional (FSANZ): https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/HealthyLiving/food-irradiation
- Proton therapy activity: half-lives of minutes to about 20 minutes (O-15, N-13, C-11), mostly gone within an hour or two: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3840408
- Photonuclear thresholds stated precisely: Be-9 1.67 MeV, deuterium 2.22 MeV, most nuclei 8 MeV or more; Co-60 below all: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photodisintegration
- "Codex" standard named (US FDA allows 7.5 MeV X-rays with some targets).
- Question premise: mould and bacteria, or stopping pests breeding (phytosanitary doses sterilise insects); a Co-60 source has no "beam" to switch off. New Zealand allows produce irradiation for pest control: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/foodtech/irradiation/Irradiation-of-fruit-and-vegetables
- Food radioactivity "mainly" from K-40 (C-14 contributes); banana 10 to 15 Bq.
- KCl demo set-up hint added: https://instructional-resources.physics.uiowa.edu/demos/7d1013-radioactive-vs-irradiated-salt
- Wording made "measurably radioactive" (Co-60 can excite rare isomers such as In-115m, negligibly).

## Verified
- Co-60 gamma energies 1.17 and 1.33 MeV.
- Codex limits: X-rays ≤ 5 MeV, electrons ≤ 10 MeV: https://www.piwet.pulawy.pl/irradiacja/codexalimentarius1.pdf
- Chernobyl I-131 (8 days) and Cs-137 (30 years).
- Medical devices sterilised by gamma or electron beam in packaging: https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/gray.pdf
- Nuclear medicine patient precautions: https://www.gosh.nhs.uk/documents/9979/Bone_scans_F0473_FINAL_Mar19.pdf
- Thermoluminescence test EN 1788 for irradiated spices.
- Natural potassium 31.7 Bq per gram: derivation.
