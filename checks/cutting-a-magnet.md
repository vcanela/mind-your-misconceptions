# Verification: Cutting a magnet

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Compass demo: attraction proves nothing (a compass attracts any steel); only repulsion shows a pole. Sewing needle or hacksaw blade recommended over paper clips: https://www.miniphysics.com/properties-of-magnets.html
- "Fields are produced by moving charges" → currents and the built-in magnetism of electrons (spin is not orbital motion).
- "Every known source is a complete magnet" → every source makes closed field lines (a straight wire has no poles).
- Electric field lines "from charges" (induced electric fields form loops); "the whole story" softened.
- Spin ice: monopoles are defects breaking the ice rule; they source H, not B, so ∇·B = 0 still holds: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1112.3793
- "Nothing in physics forbids" → "no deeper principle rules out" (Maxwell's equations as written do).
- Cabrera: event size matched the expected monopole signal (PRL 48, 1378, 1982).
- Bank cards: "many", not "old".

## Verified
- New poles at the cut; heating or hammering demagnetises; B strong inside a bar magnet; closed field lines: derivation.
- Fridge magnets striped with alternating poles: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halbach_array
- Magnetic stripes store data as flux reversals: https://www.silabs.com/documents/public/application-notes/AN148.pdf
- Earth's north geographic pole is near a magnetic south pole; field from the outer-core dynamo.
- Dirac 1931, charge quantisation (Proc. R. Soc. A 133, 60).
- Cabrera, 14 February 1982, Stanford, never repeated: https://physicstoday.aip.org/news/cabrera-counts-flux-quanta-to-find-a-dirac-monopole
- Gauss's law for magnetism: derivation.
