# Verification: The hydrogen electron

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together), with options rechecked; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Hydrogen patterns share energies (n² states per level); reworded to "only certain energies are possible".
- Stability: stationary states have no time-varying charge, and the ground state has nothing lower to drop to (excited states do radiate).
- Inside the nucleus: a probability of a few parts in 10¹⁵, not "time spent" (no trajectory).
- 2013 "quantum microscope" imaged excited Stark states via ionised electrons, not ordinary orbitals: https://physics.aps.org/articles/v6/58
- |ψ|² is a probability density; orbitals are the wavefunctions; tagline and feedback wording made consistent.
- Ring demo caveat (de Broglie ring is the Bohr picture); "shell" is legitimate terminology.
- Question: key was the odd one out; ring distractor replaced with de Broglie's circular standing wave, a genuinely taught idea.

## Verified
- Bohr 1913; E_n = −13.6/n² eV; classical collapse about 1.6 × 10⁻¹¹ s: derivation.
- Most probable radius a₀ = 0.0529 nm; |ψ₁ₛ|² maximum at the nucleus: derivation.
- Be-7 electron capture varies up to about 1% with chemistry (Ohtsuki et al. 2004).
