- "pushes them all round the loop at once" → all start moving at once; added that ions carry the current inside the cell.
- Option b: sameness of current now tied to charge not piling up, not to the definition alone.

- Drift speed restricted to the connecting wires (in a thin filament it is centimetres per second).

- Antennas and transmission lines: "change very quickly or are very long".
