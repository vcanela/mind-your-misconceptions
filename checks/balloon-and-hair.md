# Verification: Balloon and hair

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together); corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Physics error: attraction does not show opposite charge (a charged object attracts neutral ones by polarisation); only repulsion shows like charge. Feedback and demo rewritten: https://demoweb.physics.ucla.edu/node/217
- Electroscope demo now uses a known-sign charge to tell the signs apart.
- Warmth from rubbing is work transferred to internal energy, not something new.
- Equal charges "immediately afterwards"; leakage over time noted.
- Sticky tape demo procedure corrected (one strip on another, discharged, peeled together, then apart): https://www.physicsclassroom.com/lab/estatics/SE2tg.doc
- Thunderstorms: graupel and ice crystals with supercooled water, as the leading explanation.
- Electron lifetime bound attributed to Borexino and to the neutrino plus photon channel: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.01223
- What moves between insulators is still argued over (Baytekin et al., Science 2011).
- Option c: removed the unmotivated "small".

## Verified
- Contact charging without rubbing; triboelectric ordering: https://www.alphalabinc.com/triboelectric-series/
- Same-material charging (Sobarzo et al., Nature 638, 2025).
- Door handle sparks; dryer cling: derivation.
