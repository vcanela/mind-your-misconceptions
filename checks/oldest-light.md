# Verification: The oldest light

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together), with revisions rechecked; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Title and id were "Stretched light", echoing the key; renamed. Stem no longer signals "simple ... at face value".
- Relativistic-Doppler option feedback softened: the redshift can rigorously be treated as a Doppler shift along the light's path (Bunn and Hogg 2009, https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1081); what fails is reading it as the rate at which distance grows (Davis and Lineweaver, https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0310808).
- "Distances across the universe" (no galaxies at CMB release); "now more than about 14 billion light-years"; "police radar speed guns" (lidar isn't Doppler).

## Verified
- CMB z* = 1089.9, released about 380,000 years at about 3,000 K, now 2.7255 K: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.01589
- Hubble distance c/H₀ ≈ 13.4 to 14.5 Gly: derivation.
- JWST z > 14 (MoM-z14 z = 14.44): https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11263
- Supernova time dilation by (1 + z), DES 2024: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05050
