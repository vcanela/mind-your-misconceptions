Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together), with revisions rechecked; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- "c² converts mass into energy" reinforced the conversion misconception; now "a mass into the equivalent energy".
- Einstein's 1905 paper "established the general idea" (Poincaré 1900, Hasenöhrl 1904 had special cases).
- Question: both distractors were speeds and the key the only non-speed; added a genuine fourth option (kinetic energy at c).
