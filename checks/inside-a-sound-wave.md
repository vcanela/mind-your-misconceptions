# Verification: Inside a sound wave

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together); corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Displacement amplitude at conversational loudness is about 10 to 50 nm, not "a few billionths of a metre": derivation; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_displacement
- "Circles" distractor was not a documented idea; replaced with the documented wavy-path-away belief (Wittmann, Steinberg and Redish, Int. J. Sci. Educ. 2003): https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0207031
- Steel: 5,000 m/s in a thin rod, about 5,800 to 6,000 m/s in bulk: https://hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/Sound/souspe2.html
- Air lacks springiness against shear (it does resist sliding, through viscosity).
- Everyday experience "rarely" shows the motion (a candle flame by a bass speaker does).
- S-wave shadow is "key evidence" for the liquid core (Jeffreys 1926 also decisive).
- Pressure and displacement relation stated fully; "out in the open" added to the stem (reflections).

## Verified
- Longitudinal sound, compressions and rarefactions; v = √(K/ρ); air 343, water about 1,480 m/s.
- S-wave shadow beyond about 103°: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_zone
- "Pushed away" is a documented belief (over 40% in Wittmann 2003).
