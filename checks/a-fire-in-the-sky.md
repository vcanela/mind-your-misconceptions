# Verification: A fire in the sky

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together); corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Kelvin: the "nuclear energy" excuse applies to his Sun calculation only; his Earth-cooling age was flawed for other reasons (Perry 1895, convection), and the radioactivity story is a known myth: https://www.geosociety.org/gsatoday/archive/17/1/pdf/i1052-5173-17-1-4.pdf (abstract only).
- Kelvin's estimate: at most about 100 million years (1862), later 20 to 40 million; he was William Thomson then.
- Bethe and Critchfield 1938 (pp chain); Bethe alone 1939 (CNO); von Weizsäcker 1938.
- "Ten billion years" of steady shining (main sequence); "hundreds of times too long"; neutrino flux about 65 billion per cm² per second: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0312045
- Question: key was the extreme of an ordered range, and "many trillions" was not a held idea; options now a few hundred, thousand (key), million, billion years.

## Verified
- Coal Sun 4,900 years (coal 24 to 33 MJ/kg): derivation; https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/coal-heating-values-d_1675.html
- Sun's mass, luminosity, core and surface temperatures: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html ; https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/
- Fusion 0.7%; millions of times coal; oxygen about 0.6% of the Sun's mass: derivation; https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.0948
- Sun white from space: https://solar-center.stanford.edu/activities/SunColor/SunDetermine.html
- Mass loss 4.3 × 10⁹ kg/s, about 0.07% over the main sequence: derivation.
- Eddington, 24 August 1920.
