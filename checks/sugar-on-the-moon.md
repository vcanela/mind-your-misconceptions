# Verification: Sugar on the Moon

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together); corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Apollo gait: no source links the hop to reduced grip; the stiff pressurised suits and low gravity explain it (Carr and McGee, PLoS ONE 2009): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2719915 . Reworded: same sideways force needed, grip supplies only a fraction.
- Balance: only a beam balance reads the same on the Moon; an electronic balance measures force and would read a sixth (and is calibrated where used).
- Shake test: "more mass", not "heavier".
- Astronaut mass devices: SLAMMD measures acceleration; Skylab and Russian devices time spring oscillations.

## Verified
- Moon g 1.62 m/s²; 9.8 N → 1.6 N: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html
- Kilogram prototype 1889; redefinition via Planck's constant from 20 May 2019: https://www.bipm.org/en/history-si/kilogram
- g from 9.780 (equator) to 9.832 (poles): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_gravity
- SLAMMD: https://www.space.com/space-exploration/human-spaceflight/how-do-astronauts-weigh-themselves-in-space
