# Verification: Pot on the boil

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent (facts and options together); corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Question cue: both distractors said "rises", so the correct "stays" was the odd one out. Replaced one with a genuine falling distractor ("drops slightly, because faster boiling carries energy away faster"); correct option shortened.
- "Can't get hotter than its boiling point" contradicted the superheating paragraph; reworded to steady boiling.
- Boiling stops "almost at once" only on gas; electric hobs keep supplying energy for a while.
- Freezing is the reverse of melting, not boiling; reworded to condensing and freezing.
- Steam carries mostly latent energy; about 8% of L_v pushes back the atmosphere ("mostly" added).
- Steam burns compared gram for gram.
- Everest: food cooks slowly (climbers use pressure cookers), not "barely".
- Sanitiser flammability warning added.

## Verified
- L_v ≈ 2.26 MJ/kg, 6.7 times the energy to heat from 20 to 100 °C: derivation.
- Pressure cooker about 121 °C at 15 psi gauge; Everest about 72 °C at 33.7 kPa: derivation (Antoine equation).
- Microwave superheating warning (FDA notice, quoted at https://www.snopes.com/science/microwav.htm; FDA original not opened).
- Frost protection by sprinkling: https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/using_your_sprinkler_system_to_protect_blueberries_from_freezes
