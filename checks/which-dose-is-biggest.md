# Verification: Which dose is biggest?

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent against radiation-protection sources, with options rechecked; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Radon is the largest natural source worldwide on average (UNSCEAR about 1.26 of 2.4 mSv), not only in granite areas.
- EU aircrew rule: assessment for crew likely to exceed 1 mSv a year, usually by calculation; rosters and information: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2013/59/article/35
- Sievert: effective dose also weights organ sensitivity (ICRP 103).
- LNT evidence: cancer risk roughly proportional above about 100 mSv (atomic-bomb survivors).
- Chest X-ray specified as a single view (two views about 0.1 mSv).
- Banana: "about a thousand or more times"; body regulates potassium.
- Nuclear station option: "normally operating".

## Verified
- Banana 0.0001 mSv: https://ionactive.co.uk/resource-hub/guidance/radioactive-bananas-banana-equivalent-dose
- Dental 0.005, chest CT about 6 mSv: https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/safety-xray
- Near a power station < 0.01 mSv/yr: https://www.nrc.gov/waste/decommissioning/faq.html
- Auckland–London return 0.1 to 0.2 mSv (London–Sydney return 186 µSv by CARI-6): https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/946154/JSP_392_Chapter_33_-_COSMIC_RADIATION__Alt_Text_.pdf
- NZ background about 2 mSv (ESR about 1.9): https://hiddenhazards.otagomuseum.nz/hazards/radiation
- Aircrew 2 to 3 mSv/yr vs nuclear workers about 1 mSv: https://asn.fr/annual_report/2017gb/56
- Ramsar up to 260 mSv/yr; Kerala cohort found no significant excess: https://www.amrita.edu/publication/background-radiation-and-cancer-incidence-in-kerala-india-karunagappally-cohort-study/
