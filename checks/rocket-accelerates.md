# Verification: Rocket accelerates

Checked 2026-10-09 by an independent fact-checking agent; corrections applied the same day.

## Corrected
- Option a feedback said "a straight path means a constant velocity": false (speed can change along a straight line). Replaced with: a path stays straight only if the force points along it.
- Option c: "bends in the direction of the force" → "bends towards the side the force pushes on".
- Cathode ray tubes: electrostatic plates steered oscilloscope tubes; TV tubes mostly used magnetic coils. Reworded.
- Asteroids: the arcade ship has a little drag and does eventually stop; reworded.
- Skids: ice has low, not zero, friction; reworded to "almost straight".
- Lightening rocket: dropped "a little" (mass can halve during a burn).
- Circle: added that it holds ignoring fuel burn.
- Gemini 4: added McDivitt's use of nearly half his manoeuvring fuel.
